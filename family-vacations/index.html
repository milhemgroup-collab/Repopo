Provenance

Where this came from, and what it isn't

Gmail — everything

Airline confirmations, hotel folios, Airbnb messages, cancellation notices, and three emails Matt wrote himself about the Austin plan. This is the entire substantive source.

Google Calendar — nothing

Searched across the full range for vacation, trip, flight, beach and travel. Zero matching events. Not one trip in this ledger appears on a calendar.

Google Drive — almost nothing

Two PDFs of the same cancelled August 2022 Southwest booking. No other trip material exists in the Drive.

Photos — none usable

Around 120 images across two folders. One cluster predates the range; the other is a camera-roll dump. Four images date-match the St. Louis trip exactly and all four turned out to be screenshots — a rental confirmation, a work email, and a private message thread. Nothing was embedded.

Confirmation codes, ticket and loyalty numbers, card digits and email addresses were stripped before this page was written.
Dates marked approx. come from a reminder or receipt rather than a booking, so the exact nights may be off by a day.
